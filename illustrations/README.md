# Illustrations

This folder is the placeholder for the **real** illustrations of *Tesi and the Great Merge Conflict* — the ones a real illustrator would draw with real ink on real paper.

Twelve pictures, one per chapter:

| # | Chapter | Illustration idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ciao. Mamma. | Wide kitchen scene. Grey London morning through the window. Giraffe hunched at the laptop. Tesi on the horn, peering at the screen. Three empty espresso cups. |
| 2 | The Deadline | Family council around the kitchen table. Digital clock overlaid: `48:00:00`. Draghetta with her marshmallow bag. Topo with the twelve-logo scroll unrolled. |
| 3 | Draghetta's Fire | Small *fwoomph* leaving Draghetta's nostrils, laptop screen going kernel-panic black. Topo airborne toward the fruit bowl. |
| 4 | Topo Sings | The fruit bowl as stage. Topo mid-aria, paws over heart. Family in silhouette listening. Musical notes rising into the ceiling light. |
| 5 | The Merge Conflict | Split-screen: Babbo on his phone at an airport gate on the left, Mamma at the laptop on the right, both branches meeting at the top in a swirl. Tesi standing on the `8` key. |
| 6 | Babbo's Espresso | Silver tray, one espresso, thin lemon-peel curl. Babbo silhouetted in the doorway. Mamma looking up like a lighthouse spotting home. |
| 7 | Donkey Shoes | Line-up of animal feet, all wearing beautifully-fitted Donkey Shoes: donkey, horse, dog, giraffe. Mamma gesturing with a hoof, in her presentation pair. |
| 8 | Tesi Bites Mummy | Extreme close-up. One green parrotlet cheek against one enormous giraffe ear. Tiny beak, gentle chomp. Mamma's whole face laughing. |
| 9 | The Advisory Board | Formal group portrait — Tesi, Topo, Draghetta — arranged like a corporate board photo, unimpressed and dignified. |
| 10 | Andiamo | Aerial view of London street. Giraffe running in Donkey Shoes, human running alongside with a mouse-ear poking from his pocket, small dragon bringing up the rear, green dot flying above. |
| 11 | Submitted | The moment of the click. Five limbs — hoof, feather, hand, claw, paw — all overlapping on the trackpad. Screen behind reads: *Thank you.* |
| 12 | Ciao Mamma, Grazie | Big soft chair by the window. Family piled together sleeping. Tesi puffed up on Mamma's chest, over her heart. Golden streetlamp rectangle on the carpet. |

Until the real ones arrive, each chapter closes with a small ASCII drawing embedded in the text.
