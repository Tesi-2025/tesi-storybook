# Chapter 9 — The Advisory Board

There was, in the YC application form, a small field. It read:

> **Please list any advisors, mentors, or people who have materially helped shape the company.**

Mamma stared at the field. She turned to the kitchen. The kitchen turned, more or less, to her.

"*Allora,*" she said. "*Il consiglio di amministrazione.*"

The advisory board.

Tesi, from her shoulder, ruffled importantly. Topo, from the counter, stood on his hind legs and adjusted an imaginary bow tie. Draghetta, from the floor, sat up very straight and folded her wings behind her back the way a small dragon does when she is about to be Officially Recognised.

Babbo, drying his mane with a towel from the hallway, said, "You can't put them on the form, *amore.*"

"Why not?"

"Because they will think you are joking."

"*Ma non sto scherzando.*"

Mamma turned back to the form. She thought about it. She thought about all the meetings the advisory board had actually attended. She thought about the time Draghetta had, unprompted, drawn a competitive-landscape matrix on the kitchen wall in coloured chalk, correctly identifying every single competitor including the Australian one everybody else forgets. She thought about the time Topo, during a customer interview, had sung the Donkey Shoes value proposition in three-part harmony (Topo can do three-part harmony alone, this is a mouse trade secret), causing the customer to cry and immediately sign a letter of intent. She thought about the merge conflict yesterday, and the way Tesi had hopped onto the number eight, and how Tesi had never once — *never once* — advised her wrongly.

She began to type.

> **Advisory board:**
>
> - **Tesi** — Pacific parrotlet, CIBS-certified. Bug detection, git conflict resolution, executive interruption of unproductive thought loops. Attended every founding meeting since month one.
> - **Topo** — mouse, coloratura tenor. Customer empathy specialist. Assets and design. Has independently closed one letter of intent via operatic aria.
> - **Draghetta** — dragon, junior. Competitive analysis, technical debugging (thermal method), morale (marshmallow logistics). Currently learning fire safety in shared spaces.

She read it back. She looked at Babbo.

"*I put them on.*"

Babbo laughed, the deep gentle laugh of a man who has stopped being surprised by his wife long ago and now simply enjoys watching her be exactly herself.

"*Amore, if they don't take you because of the parrotlet, they don't deserve you.*"

Mamma nodded. She saved the form.

Then, in a small ceremony that took approximately thirty seconds, she officially appointed each of them. She held out her hoof, one at a time. Tesi hopped onto it. Topo climbed onto it. Draghetta, gently, placed one small clawed paw on it. And Mamma said, in her most solemn giraffe voice:

"*Tesi, Topo, Draghetta — vi nomino ufficialmente advisory board di Donkey Shoes. Che Dio ci aiuti tutti.*"

Then she gave each of them a small treat: a millet spray for Tesi, a piece of parmesan for Topo, and a marshmallow for Draghetta, which Draghetta briefly toasted to golden-brown perfection before eating.

The advisory board accepted its role with dignity.
