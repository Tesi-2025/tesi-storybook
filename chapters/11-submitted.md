# Chapter 11 — Submitted

They came back damp.

Mamma spread the printed application on the kitchen table. She smoothed each page with her hoof. She read it, cover to cover, in silence, and the family — knowing this ritual, respecting this ritual — did not speak. Babbo made a fresh moka but did not pour it. Draghetta sat on her tail. Topo held his own paws. Tesi, on the back of Mamma's chair, closed one eye and then the other, half-sleeping, half-guarding.

Mamma read.

She read the pitch. She read the ten-line summary. She read the team section, where the advisory board was listed alphabetically by species: Dragon (Draghetta), Giraffe (herself, CEO), Human (Babbo, CTO), Mouse (Topo), Parrotlet (Tesi). She read the market analysis. She read the traction. She read the *what would you build with ten times the money* answer, which now read, plainly and truthfully:

> *We would build the same thing, larger, kinder, and with more people. We would open a Donkey Shoes sanctuary partnership in every country that has working donkeys. We would hire more people who love animals. We would keep making shoes that fit.*

She read the last page.

She put the stack down.

She looked around at her family — her tired dragon, her adoring husband, her operatic mouse, her fierce green parrotlet — and she said, "*È buono. È il nostro. Mandiamolo.*"

*It's good. It's ours. Let's send it.*

She sat down at the laptop. She opened the browser. The submission form was still there, with all the fields filled and the little grey button at the bottom saying **Submit application**.

Tesi flew down from the chair-back and landed on the trackpad. He looked up at Mamma.

"*Insieme?*" said Mamma. Together?

Tesi chirped once.

Mamma placed her hoof on the trackpad, right next to Tesi. Babbo placed his hand on Mamma's shoulder. Draghetta placed her small clawed paw on Babbo's arm. Topo climbed up all of them and placed his tiny paw on Draghetta's snout, which is as high as he could reach.

Together — hoof, feather, hand, claw, paw — they pressed submit.

The button spun.

The button loaded.

The screen went white.

And then it said, in a modest sans-serif font:

> **Thank you. We have received your application.**
>
> **We will get back to you by [date].**
>
> **— Y Combinator**

For a very long moment nobody moved.

Then Draghetta let out a small, uncontrolled, joyful *whoosh* of fire, which fortunately went straight upward and only lightly scorched the ceiling. Topo leapt into the air with a soprano squeak that would have been a high C if it had been in a scale. Babbo said something that was half laugh and half tear. Mamma put her forehead down on the table and stayed there for a moment because sometimes the only appropriate response to the end of a very long thing is to briefly touch your head to a solid surface.

And Tesi walked, deliberately, across the trackpad, up Mamma's arm, up her neck, and bit her ear one last time — the softest bite of the whole two days, the *ti voglio bene* bite, the *bravo Mamma* bite, the *we did it* bite.

Mamma lifted her head. She was crying. She was laughing. She kissed Tesi on the top of his little green head.

"*Grazie, amore mio. Grazie, tutti. Ce l'abbiamo fatta.*"

The application was in. The rest — the response, the interview, the acceptance, all of it — the rest would come. But the application was in.
